By Brian Eno ([src](https://mattrickard.com/list-of-all-oblique-strategies))

> These cards evolved from our separate
> observations on the principles underlying
> what we were doing. Sometimes they were
> recognized in retrospect
> (intellect catching up with intuition),
> sometimes they were identified as they were
> happening, sometimes they were formulated.

> They can be used as a pack
> (a set of possibilities being continuously reviewed
> in the mind) or by drawing a single card from the
> shuffled pack when a dilemma occurs in a working
> situation. In this case, the card is trusted even
> if its appropriateness is quite unclear. They are
> not final, as new ideas will present themselves,
> and others will become self-evident.
