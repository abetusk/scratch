490643 method
---

### Materials

* Linen cover stock (67lbs)
  - Southwork 25^ cotton, 65lbs, 8 1/2 x 11 ([amzn](https://www.amazon.com/Southworth-Cotton-Business-Coverstock-Z550CK/dp/B00125JBX4))
* spray adhesive
* rolling pin
* craft knife or rotary cutter
* metal ruler
* cutting mat
* corner punch
* light table
* clear acrylic spray ([amzn](https://www.amazon.com/Krylon-K01303007-Acrylic-Crystal-11-Ounce/dp/B00023JE7K)?)

### Instructions

* Linen card stock has a printing surface that needs to be set to make sure the print is on the appropriate side
* Print both front and back
* If printing on inkjet, coat front and back ink with multiple coats of clear acrylic spray
* Spray adhesive on *one* back-side (not both), align and press
  - align pieces over light table to make sure alignment is correct
  - put protective sheet above print so roller won't damage art
  - press hard
  - 1hr+ of heavy books as an alternative
* Cut cards out of sheet
* Punch cut corners of cards
